05 Good questions, clear answers

Before you
take the leap.

Can I study online or on campus?+

Catalyst is planned as a hybrid learning centre, with an on-campus location in F-10, Islamabad, and online learning for students elsewhere. Confirmed delivery options may vary by course.

What can I study at Catalyst?+

Example learning paths include AI and SaaS, Forex, Crypto, and AI-powered content creation. Final course outlines and schedules are being prepared.

How are Quran studies and ethics included?+

Quran studies are a compulsory component for Muslim students. Ethics is part of the learning experience for all students, Muslim and non-Muslim.

Where can I find fees and start dates?+

Fees and confirmed dates have not been provided yet. The calendar currently uses clearly marked examples; contact admissions for verified information when it becomes available.
